NoBSPDFeditor — Guide
Opening Files
Open PDFDrag & drop a PDF on the landing screen, or click Choose File. You can also click Open PDF in the toolbar to swap to a new file.
Insert PDFClick Insert PDF to append another PDF's pages to the end of the current document.
Navigation
Prev / NextUse the Prev and Next buttons in the top bar to move between pages.
Sidebar thumbnailsClick any thumbnail in the left sidebar to jump directly to that page.
Reorder pagesDrag a thumbnail up or down in the sidebar to rearrange pages.
Color & Brush
Color pickerClick the color swatch in the toolbar to open the full color picker.
Color hotbarQuickly switch between saved colors. The first two slots are presets. Click an empty slot to save the current color there.
Brush sizeDrag the slider to change the draw/eraser stroke width.
Page Management
Delete PageClick Delete Page in the toolbar to permanently remove the current page.
Extract pagesSelect pages in the sidebar (see below), then click the Extract button that appears to save them as a separate PDF.
Sidebar Multi-Select
Single clickNavigate to a page and select it.
Ctrl + clickToggle individual pages in or out of the selection without deselecting others.
Shift + clickSelect a contiguous range of pages from the last clicked page.
Keyboard Shortcuts
Ctrl + ZUndo
Ctrl + Shift + ZRedo
Ctrl + FOpen search bar
Enter (in search)Next result
Shift + Enter (in search)Previous result
EscClose search / close modal
Enter (in signature modal)Add signature
Saving & Printing
DownloadClick Download to save the annotated PDF to your device.
PrintClick Print to send the annotated PDF to your printer.
